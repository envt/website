Emilien Vallot

Senior / Lead Software Engineer with 17 years of professional experience building C++ software, 3D graphics applications, and tools across Windows, Linux, and macOS.

about

Passionate about developing and automating things. It all started back in 2003.

At that time, I was already programming in assembly to make 4 levels of gray drawings on my HP40g calculator, but I wanted to become a 3D graphic designer.

- spoiler alert -

Unfortunately, it didn't work out and I thought to myself: Maybe I can develop tools for graphic designers?!

This explains my background, focused on digital imaging, computer vision, and later virtual reality.

Since then, I've been developing software and tools in several industries such as film, architecture, videography, robotics, or even professional audio.

I'm always looking for new challenges and things to learn.

When I'm not coding, I also enjoy reading, going to the movies, drawing, juggling, and photography!

career

All these years allowed me to gather a wide range of skills. I also keep ending up in large, messy codebases — cleanup, architecture, tooling, the refactor that lets the product keep shipping.

My first job was at TechViz. A team of about ten, putting 3D software into CAVEs and VR. That's where C, C++, OpenGL, GLSL, linear algebra, networking, and hardware really clicked: the engine, the tools, customer installs, tracking devices. Seven years — an excellent first experience.

I then left for SolidAnim, a bit of a dream job: cinema, working closely with graphic designers, helping them get their job done. Film sets, real-time camera tracking, C++, Qt, maths, multi-threading, and an R&D environment where we had real freedom on the technical choices.

SolidAnim's relocation brought me to L-Acoustics. They make loudspeakers and amplifiers for the world's biggest live shows. I worked with people who were always after the next thing, modernized my C++, CMake and Qt, and spent a lot of time on control software that cannot crash during a concert — including a careful introduction of Qt into JUCE, so both could live in the same app.

After a few years came a chance to go back to rendering, at Abvent. The R&D team had full ownership of the technical choices, and we started a next-gen Artlantis on a single Mac/Windows codebase: architecture, CI, tests. I drove that for a year, and I enjoyed it.

And here we are. I am currently at Fuzzy Logic Robotics. A few years making the 3D simulation product production-grade, then a pivot to automated welding. We built a prototype that helped raise funds; now we are bringing in AI and better algorithms to make the welds more precise.
