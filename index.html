career

Throughout my 17+ years in software engineering, I have focused on bridging low-level system performance with complex 3D graphics and real-world hardware integrations.

My career started at TechViz, optimizing custom C-based OpenGL intercept drivers for CAVE setups and multi-display VR installations. I then moved to SolidAnim to develop real-time camera tracking (SLAM) solutions and virtual production plugins for feature film sets.

At L-Acoustics, I engineered high-reliability pro-audio control platforms (Network Manager) and spatial audio 3D venue simulators, designing hybrid JUCE/Qt architectures that guaranteed zero-downtime execution.

Later at Abvent, I served as lead architect unifying two legacy OS-specific codebases into a modern C++/Qt application for photorealistic rendering software (Artlantis).

After contributing to GoPro's shared core video engine toolchains, I joined Fuzzy Logic Robotics as acting tech lead. There, I architected from scratch an end-to-end automated robotic welding platform combining 3D vision, synthetic data generation, and custom container orchestration.
